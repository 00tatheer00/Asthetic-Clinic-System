# Brimish Skin Care Clinic — Clinic Staff User Guide

Welcome to the Brimish Skin Care Clinic Operating Platform! This guide explains how to use your daily dashboard simply and efficiently.

---

## 1. Quick Start & Logging In

1. Open your web browser (Chrome, Edge, or Safari) and go to:  
   **`https://brimishskincare.com/auth/login`** (or `http://localhost:3000/auth/login` locally)
2. Enter your clinic email address (e.g. `bilal@admin.com` for Dr. Bilal / Super Admin) and your password.
3. Click **Sign In**.
4. You will automatically land on the **Clinic Dashboard**.

---

## 2. Receptionist Daily Guide

### 2.1 Starting Your Day
- At the top of your dashboard, you will see **Today's Appointments**, **Pending Orders**, and **Low Stock Alerts**.
- Check the **Follow-Up Queue** for any patients scheduled for a courtesy call after treatments.

### 2.2 Finding or Adding a Patient
- **Fast Search**: Use the search bar at the top of the dashboard. Type the patient's name or mobile number.
- **Add New Patient**:
  1. Click **Add Patient** on the dashboard (or go to **Patients** in the left menu > **New Patient**).
  2. Enter their Full Name, Phone Number, and Gender.
  3. If they have medical allergies or skin sensitivities, note them in the notes box.
  4. Click **Save Patient**.

### 2.3 Managing Appointments
1. Click **Appointments** in the left menu.
2. You will see all appointments for today organized by time.
3. When a patient arrives at the clinic:
   - Click their appointment and change the status from **Confirmed** to **Checked In**.
   - The doctor will immediately see that the patient is waiting in the reception lounge.
4. If a patient calls to reschedule or cancel:
   - Click the appointment > Choose **Reschedule** or **Cancel** with a brief reason.

### 2.4 Point of Sale (POS) & Billing
1. Click **POS** in the left menu (or click **New Sale** on the dashboard).
2. **Select Patient**: Type the patient's name or phone number.
3. **Add Items**:
   - Click on the treatment performed (e.g. HydraFacial Deluxe).
   - Click on any skincare products they are purchasing (e.g. Niacinamide Serum).
4. **Discounts**: If the doctor approved a discount, enter the percentage or flat rupee amount.
5. **Payment Method**: Select **Cash**, **Card**, or **Bank Transfer**.
6. Click **Complete Sale**.
7. An official clinic tax receipt appears on screen:
   - Click **Print 80mm Receipt** for the thermal receipt printer, or **Print A4** for full letterhead paper.

### 2.5 Checking Website Product Orders
1. Click **Orders** in the left menu.
2. Orders placed by website customers appear as **Received**.
3. When you have packaged the products, update the order status to **Ready** (for pickup) or **Shipped** (for courier delivery).
4. When the customer receives their package, mark it as **Delivered**.

## 3. Doctor & Medical Director Guide

As the Doctor / Super Admin, you have access to all front-desk features plus confidential clinical records and clinic management tools.

### 3.1 Documenting a Patient Visit & Clinical Notes
1. Open the patient's profile from the **Patients** menu.
2. Click **Record New Visit**.
3. Select the treatment performed and any clinical consumables used.
4. In the **Doctor Clinical Notes** field, enter confidential diagnostic notes, skin assessment, machine settings, and prescription recommendations.
   - *Privacy Guarantee*: Receptionists cannot see your clinical notes. They remain strictly confidential between you and the patient record.
5. Set a **Follow-Up Date** if the patient requires a healing check in 3 to 7 days.

### 3.2 Before & After Gallery Cases
1. Go to **Gallery** in the left menu.
2. Click **Add New Case**.
3. Select the patient and treatment.
4. Upload the **Before** photograph and **After** photograph.
5. Check the box **Consent Obtained** (patient signed photo consent form).
6. Click **Publish Case**.
   - *Safety Rule*: If consent is ever revoked by the patient, uncheck the consent box and the photo will immediately disappear from the public website.

### 3.3 Approving Patient Testimonials (Reviews)
1. Go to **Reviews** in the left menu.
2. Read newly submitted testimonials.
3. Click **Approve** to display them on the website homepage, or **Reject** to hide them.

### 3.4 Reports & Analytics
1. Go to **Reports** in the left menu.
2. Filter by **Today**, **7 Days**, **30 Days**, or **All Time**.
3. Review total sales, treatment revenue, retail sales, and appointment completion rates.
4. Click **Download CSV** to save full accounting spreadsheets for your records.
